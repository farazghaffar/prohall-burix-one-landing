How to use

Your Burix One routine in eight steps

Heat and the number of flat-iron passes decide your finish.

  1. Prepare

    Wash 2–3 times with shampoo only, no conditioner. Finish with a filtered-water rinse.

  2. Dry to 50%

    Section the hair and blow dry until about halfway dry, still slightly damp.

  3. Apply

    Use a plastic bowl, never metal. In 4 sections, brush on from mid-length to ends, ½ in (1 cm) from the scalp. Start with less.

  4. Process

    Cover with a shower cap and wait 60–90 minutes. Thick or very curly hair needs the full 90.

  5. Rinse

    Rinse with cool water and good pressure until hair feels completely clean.

  6. Dry 100%

    Dry completely to the ends. Stretch with a brush for a straighter finish.

  7. Flat iron

    Thin sections. Fine 375–400°F, 10–12 passes · normal 450°F, 15–20 · thick 450–460°F, 20–25. Curls: 1–2 light passes. Natural: skip the iron.

  8. Balance

    Rinse with cool water, apply Prohall Equalize Mask to mid-lengths and ends for 10 minutes, rinse and style.

Safety: should be applied by a trained stylist or under professional supervision. Always test on a small strand first. External use only; keep away from children and avoid eye contact. If stinging or discomfort occurs, stop and rinse with plenty of water; consult a doctor if irritation persists. On bleached or sensitized hair, keep the iron at or below 355°F (180°C). FAQ

Questions, answered

Will my hair be completely straight?

Only if you want it to be. Heat and the number of flat-iron passes control the finish, from natural movement to sleek and straight.

Will it change my hair color?

It can lighten your shade by up to two levels. If that is not wanted, plan your color for the same day, right after the treatment.

Can I color my hair the same day?

Yes. Burix One and hair color work well together, so color can be applied immediately afterwards.

When can I wash my hair?

Whenever you like. There is no waiting period; the same day is fine.

How much do I need?

Short to medium hair usually needs 2 to 3 oz; long or thick hair, 3 to 4 oz. Start with less and add as you go.

Do I need special products afterwards?

Sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner, plus a heat protectant whenever you blow dry or flat iron. A pH-balancing mask after any chemical service also helps hold the result.
